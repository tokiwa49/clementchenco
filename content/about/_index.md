Hey. This is Clement, a venture builder and investor working across deep tech, infrastructure, and digital assets.

The infrastructure on the ground: the road, the highway, the street lamps, these are all repeatable and reusable by default. But it was never the case in orbit. Everything in space is still being built a single mission at a time.

I'm currently co-founder of [Satelyx](https://satelyx.com/), and this is at the core of what we are building: a repeatable orbital infrastructure. We offer In-Orbit-Demonstration as a Service, and use that to offer capabilities on demand and intelligence on demand, offering a simple plug-in into a responsive space.

Before building, I spent years as a venture investor where I have the amazing opportunities to look closely into hundreds of startups across tech, infrastructure, and digital assets. I hold a BBA in Finance from NTU in Taiwan.

I also had experiences working with the broader finance industry, from FOREX trading to algorithmic trading. I also enjoy building things with code.

This is where I share what I'm building, what I'm passionate about, and my learning along the way.
