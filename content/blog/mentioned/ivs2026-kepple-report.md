Kepple's recap of IVS2026 in Kyoto, three days, 13,000 attendees, 340 startups, singled out Satelyx among the LAUNCHPAD international finalists. The report covers what we pitched: helping private space companies get through the valley of death between a technology that works on paper and one that's actually flown, by offering satellite-based testing as a service. Our first satellite launch, using Japanese propulsion, is targeted for late 2027.

<!--more-->

Space Quarters took the LAUNCHPAD grand prize this year, with Akari Hosho and MUSE rounding out the top three; the report has the fuller list of who was there and what else the three days covered, from MOF research commercialization to Kyoto's manufacturing ecosystem.

We were named a LAUNCHPAD finalist the month before; see [that announcement](/blog/mentioned/ivs2026-launchpad-finalist/) for the full finalist list.

Full report (Japanese): [Kepple, July 9, 2026](https://kepple.co.jp/articles/3zobztr9n).
